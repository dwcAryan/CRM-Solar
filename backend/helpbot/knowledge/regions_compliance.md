# Multi-Country Support (India and Australia)

The CRM dynamically adapts fields, currencies, tax structures, and terminologies based on the lead's country: India (IN) or Australia (AU).

## India (IN) Configuration
- **Currency**: Indian Rupee (INR ₹)
- **Phone Prefix**: +91 (10-digit mobile)
- **Utility Provider**: DISCOM (Electricity Distribution Company, e.g., TNEB, MSEDCL, Tata Power)
- **Incentive Terminology**: "Government Subsidy" (e.g., PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana)
- **Tax Terminology**: GST (Goods & Services Tax)
- **Preferred Channels**: WhatsApp first, then SMS / Phone Call

## Australia (AU) Configuration
- **Currency**: Australian Dollar (AUD $)
- **Phone Prefix**: +61 (9-digit mobile / 10 with leading 0)
- **Utility Provider**: Electricity Retailer / DNSP (e.g., Origin Energy, AGL, EnergyAustralia, Ausgrid)
- **Incentive Terminology**: "STC Rebate / Government Incentive"
- **Tax Terminology**: GST (10%)
- **Preferred Channels**: SMS first, then Phone Call / WhatsApp
- **Do-Not-Call Compliance**: Strictly honors DNCR registration and opt-out requests.

> **Disclaimer**: System settings provide operational controls, but the business must verify applicable legal, tax, and licensing regulations with a qualified advisor in each jurisdiction.
