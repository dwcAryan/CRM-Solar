# Product Tour, Tour Center, and Keyboard Shortcuts

Solar CRM features an interactive Driver.js product tour and keyboard shortcuts to help team members quickly master the software.

## Product Tour Features
1. **Interactive Spotlight**: Step-by-step highlighted walk-through of the main navigation, pipeline views, lead interaction panel, and quotes engine.
2. **Tour Center**: Open the Tour Center anytime from the top Help icon or your User Profile menu to take specialized 1-minute topic tours:
   - Full Overview Tour
   - Leads & Pipeline Walkthrough
   - Site Surveys & Mobile Uploads
   - Quotes & Pricing Calculation
   - CSV Lead Import & Distribution
   - AI Suggestions & Call Retries
3. **Completion & Replay**: You can retake any tour anytime without losing your place.

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- **`?` (Question Mark)**: Instantly open the Help Assistant Chatbot from any screen.
- **`Esc`**: Dismiss the Product Tour or close the Help Chatbot modal.
- **`Left / Right Arrows`**: Navigate previous and next steps in the active tour.
