# Generating Quotes and Proposals

The Quotes module calculates solar system sizing, equipment costs, government subsidies/rebates, and produces branded PDF proposals.

## Quote Calculation Workflow
1. **System Sizing**: Select system capacity in kilowatts (e.g., 3 kW, 5 kW, 10 kW) based on customer's monthly power bill and roof space.
2. **Component Selection**:
   - **Solar Panels**: Monocrystalline PERC, TOPCon, Bifacial panels.
   - **Inverter**: On-Grid String inverter, Hybrid inverter, or Microinverters.
   - **Battery Storage**: Optional lithium battery backup (e.g., 5 kWh, 10 kWh).
3. **Financials & Incentives**:
   - **Gross System Price**: Base equipment and installation charges.
   - **Incentives / Subsidies**:
     - *India (IN)*: Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy applied automatically based on capacity tiers.
     - *Australia (AU)*: STC (Small-scale Technology Certificates) rebate discount deducted from upfront price.
   - **Net Payable**: Gross Price minus Subsidies minus approved discounts plus applicable GST/tax.
4. **Sending Proposal**: Salespeople can generate a PDF, download it, or send it directly via Email or WhatsApp to the customer with one click.
