# Notifications and Alerts

Solar CRM delivers real-time notifications to keep team members informed of critical updates without delay.

## Types of Notifications
1. **Lead Assignment**: When a manager or round-robin assigns a new lead to you.
2. **Follow-Up Reminders**: 15 minutes before a scheduled call or callback.
3. **Survey Scheduled / Completed**: Alerts the surveyor when a site visit is assigned, and alerts the salesperson when inspection photos/reports are submitted.
4. **Quote Feedback**: Notifies salesperson when a customer views or accepts an online proposal.

## Managing Notifications
- **Notification Bell**: Located in the top header. Displays unread count badge.
- **Mark as Read**: Clicking a notification opens the related lead/survey and clears the unread badge.
- **Web Push Permissions**: Click the "Enable Notifications" prompt in the header or sidebar to receive browser alerts even when the CRM tab is in the background.
