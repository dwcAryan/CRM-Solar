# The CRM Golden Rule

Every customer interaction MUST be logged in the CRM immediately with a specific Call Outcome and detailed notes.

## Key Principles
1. **Never leave a lead hanging**: Every lead must have either a scheduled follow-up activity, an active pipeline stage (Survey, Quote, Negotiation), or a resolved outcome (Won or Lost).
2. **Log Immediately After Calling**: Do not wait until the end of the day to batch-log calls. Timely logging ensures team members, managers, and AI assistants have up-to-the-minute status.
3. **Always Record Next Action**: When a customer asks for a callback, immediately schedule a follow-up task with the promised date and time.
4. **Honesty in Outcomes**: Never mark an unanswered call as connected or callback. Use the accurate outcome so the automated retry ladder operates correctly.
