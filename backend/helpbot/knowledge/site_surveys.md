# Site Survey Management

A technical site survey is required to evaluate rooftop feasibility, solar panel layout, electrical meter connection, and shading before finalizing a binding proposal.

## Scheduling a Survey
- When a lead reaches "Site Survey Requested", a Salesperson or Manager schedules the survey date and selects an available **Surveyor**.
- The surveyor receives an instant notification (in-app and push) with site details, customer address, and phone number.

## The Surveyor Checklist
Surveyors complete their work using the dedicated **Surveyor Workspace**:
1. **Roof Information**: Structure (RCC flat roof, tin/metal sheet, clay tile, asbestos), tilt angle, and orientation (South/North facing).
2. **Shading Analysis**: Any trees, water tanks, or nearby buildings casting shadows between 9 AM and 4 PM.
3. **Electrical Inspection**: Meter connection type (Single Phase / 3 Phase), Sanctioned Load (kW), consumer meter number.
4. **Photo Uploads**: High-resolution photos of rooftop, distribution board (DB), meter box, and inverter mounting wall.
5. **Report Submission**: Once completed, the survey is submitted and automatically transitions the lead to "Survey Completed", notifying the salesperson to prepare the final quote.
